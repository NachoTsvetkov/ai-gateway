# Email Template: Sending the Personalized AI Opportunity Report

This email template follows Daniel Priestley’s philosophy:
- Deliver value first
- Sell the **next step** (the meeting), not the service
- Use enrollment language
- Keep it short and professional

---

## Subject Line Options

Choose one of the following:

- `Your Personalized AI Opportunity Report for [Business Name]`
- `Ideas to help [Business Name] save time and grow`
- `Quick thoughts on AI for [Business Name]`

---

## Email Body

```markdown
Hi [First Name],

Thank you for filling out the questionnaire. I’ve reviewed your answers and put together a short **Personalized AI Opportunity Report** based on your business and goals.

You can find it attached to this email.

In the report, I’ve outlined a few areas where AI and automation could potentially help [Business Name], along with some initial ideas that might be worth exploring.

If any of this resonates with you, I’d be happy to jump on a quick 15–20 minute call to walk through the ideas and see if there’s a good fit to go deeper.

Would you be open to that?

Just reply to this email or use this link to book a time that works for you:  
[Insert Calendly Link]

Looking forward to hearing your thoughts.

Best regards,  
Nacho Tsvetkov  
[Your Phone Number]  
[Your Website]
```

---

## Usage Notes

- **Personalize** the first paragraph when possible (reference something specific from their survey).
- Keep the email relatively short.
- Attach the report as a **PDF** (recommended).
- The goal of this email is **not to sell** — it’s to deliver value and get them to book a call.
- The real selling should happen during the discovery call.
