# 15-Minute Mobile Checkout Audit

**Instructions:** Open your store on your phone (Safari or Chrome, not desktop preview). Use your **best-selling SKU** or the product your ads send traffic to. Score each item **0–2**:

- **0** = Broken or missing  
- **1** = Partially there  
- **2** = Clearly working  

Target: **24+ total** out of 30. Anything scoring 0–1 becomes this week's fix.
